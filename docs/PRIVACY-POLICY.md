# Privacy Policy – Return Tracker

**Effective date:** 18 November 2026  
**Last updated:** 18 November 2026  
**App name:** Return Tracker  
**Android package name:** `com.triplelimesaver.returntracker`  
**Data controller / developer:** Triple Lime Saver LTD  
**Privacy contact:** dhiraj.jha@triplelimesaver.co.uk  
**Website:** https://triplelimesaver.co.uk/

This Privacy Policy explains how Return Tracker handles information when you use the Android application. It is intended to describe the app's offline-first purchase and receipt tracking features and its use of Google services for advertising and in-app purchases.

Please read this policy before using the app. If you have questions, contact us using the email address above.

## 1. About Return Tracker

Return Tracker helps users record purchases, keep receipt copies, and monitor return deadlines and warranty dates. Depending on the version installed, features may include:

- Manually entered purchase details, including product name, retailer, amount, currency and purchase date.
- Return deadlines and warranty expiry dates.
- Receipt photographs, images and PDF attachments.
- Notes, reminders, purchase history and reports.
- CSV export and backup/restore.
- Optional advertising and purchases through Google Play.

The app is designed to work primarily on your Android device. It does not require an account or an app-operated cloud service **if the released version contains no account or backend functionality**. See the pre-publication verification checklist supplied with this file: this statement must be checked against the final source code before publication.

## 2. Information you enter or store

Depending on the features you use, Return Tracker may process the following information:

### 2.1 Purchase information

You may choose to enter information such as:

- Product or item name.
- Retailer or seller name.
- Purchase date.
- Purchase price and ISO currency code.
- Return-by date and warranty expiry date.
- Notes or other details you choose to add.

This information is used to display your purchase records, calculate and show relevant dates, organise purchase history, and generate reports or exports.

### 2.2 Receipts and attachments

If you use receipt capture or import, the app may access an image or PDF that you choose to take or select. Receipt files may contain personal information printed by a retailer, such as your name, address, loyalty details, transaction details or partial payment-card information.

Receipt files and their associated records are intended to be stored in the app's private device storage. The app does not need access to your entire photo library when you use Android's system photo picker. Camera access is used only when you choose to take a receipt photograph.

**Do not add full payment-card numbers, security codes, passwords or other information that is not needed to track a purchase.**

### 2.3 Preferences and app settings

The app may store settings you select, such as preferred currency, theme, reminder preferences and other app configuration. These settings are used to personalise the app and are stored locally unless the final implementation states otherwise.

### 2.4 Information you choose to include in exports

When you export a CSV file or create a backup, the exported file may contain the purchase information, notes and attachments included in the export. You decide where to save or share that file.

## 3. How information is stored and used

### 3.1 Local storage

Return Tracker is designed to store purchase records, app preferences and receipt attachments locally on your device. Local database and file storage are used to provide the app's core features.

We use this information to:

- Display and organise purchases.
- Show return and warranty deadlines.
- Schedule reminders you request.
- Display purchase history and reports.
- Create exports and backups when you request them.
- Maintain your app preferences.

We do not use your receipt contents or purchase records to build advertising profiles.

### 3.2 Reminders and notifications

If you enable reminders, the app may schedule local notifications for return deadlines, warranty dates or other reminders you configure. Notification scheduling may use Android background-work facilities.

These reminders are generated by the app on your device. The app may need notification permission on supported Android versions. You can manage notification permission and app notifications through Android Settings and the app's own settings, where available.

### 3.3 Currency and financial information

The app may store each purchase amount together with its original currency code. Changing your preferred currency setting does not, by itself, convert the value or change the original currency of an existing purchase. Reports should keep different currencies separate unless an actual conversion feature is provided and clearly identified.

Return Tracker is a record-keeping tool. It does not provide banking, payment-account or financial-advice services.

## 4. Camera, photos and files

The app may request or use access to:

- **Camera:** to take a photograph of a receipt when you choose this feature.
- **Photos or documents:** to let you select an existing receipt image or PDF.
- **Files or document storage:** to save or restore a backup or export when you choose that action.

Access is used to provide the feature you requested. The app should use Android's system pickers where available and should not request broad storage access unless it is necessary and justified by the implemented feature.

You can refuse a permission or cancel a file selection. Some features may then be unavailable.

## 5. Backup, restore and exports

If backup and restore are available in your version of the app:

- A backup may contain purchase records, preferences and receipt attachments, depending on the backup options you select.
- The app may create a ZIP archive and may offer password-protected encryption if that feature is enabled in the released version.
- You choose where the backup is saved and whether to transfer it to another location or service.
- If you share or upload a backup using another app or cloud-storage provider, that provider's own privacy policy and security practices apply.
- We do not receive a copy of a backup merely because you create or save it.

Keep backup files secure. Anyone who obtains an unencrypted backup, or an encrypted backup together with its password, may be able to access its contents. If password-protected backups are offered, keep the password safe: we may not be able to recover a forgotten password.

Deleting a record in the app does not automatically delete copies previously exported, shared, or saved outside the app.

## 6. Advertising and Google AdMob

The app may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Advertising services are provided by Google and may involve the collection or processing of information by Google and its partners.

Depending on the SDK version, your consent choices, device settings and the advertising configuration, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process information such as:

- IP address and approximate location inferred from it.
- Device or advertising identifiers.
- App interactions, such as ad views and taps.
- Diagnostic and performance information.
- Information used for advertising delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and service operation.

This information is processed by Google under Google's applicable terms and privacy information. The app does not intentionally send the contents of your receipts, your manually entered purchase descriptions, or your purchase notes to AdMob for advertising.

Where required, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) or another applicable consent mechanism to present privacy choices and obtain or manage consent for advertising technologies. Available choices may depend on your location and applicable law. You can also review Google's advertising and privacy controls.

Google privacy information:
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Google Ads settings: https://myadcenter.google.com/

The exact data processed depends on the AdMob SDK version, enabled features, mediation partners (if any), and consent configuration in the released app. We will update this policy if those integrations change.

## 7. Google Play Billing and purchases

The app may offer in-app purchases through Google Play Billing, including:

- A one-time purchase to remove advertisements.
- Monthly or yearly premium subscriptions.
- Optional support purchases.

Payments are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your full payment-card number or payment security code through the app.

The app may receive and process purchase-related information needed to provide and restore entitlements, such as the product identifier, purchase status, purchase token or order-related reference, and subscription status. Google processes payment and transaction information under its own terms and privacy policy.

We use purchase information to determine whether an entitlement is active, restore eligible purchases, remove advertisements when applicable, and provide premium features. Optional support purchases do not, by themselves, grant premium access or remove advertisements.

Google Play terms and privacy information:
- Google Payments Privacy Notice: https://payments.google.com/payments/apis-secure/get_legal_document?ldo=0&ldt=privacynotice
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 8. Information sharing

We do not sell your purchase records, receipt contents or notes.

Information may be processed or shared in the following circumstances, depending on the features enabled in the version you use:

- **Google AdMob:** technical, device, advertising and usage information may be processed to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and operate advertising services.
- **Google Play Billing:** purchase and transaction information is processed by Google to complete purchases and manage entitlements.
- **Services you choose:** if you export, share or upload a file using another app or service, information in that file is provided to the service you selected and is governed by that provider's terms.
- **Legal and safety reasons:** information may be disclosed where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety and security.

We do not disclose receipt contents or manually entered purchase details to advertising providers as an intended use of the app.

## 9. Data retention and deletion

### 9.1 Data stored on your device

Purchase records, preferences and receipt files stored locally remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app's data, uninstall the app, or the relevant Android storage is otherwise removed. The exact behaviour can depend on your Android version, device backup settings and the app implementation.

You can delete individual purchase records and associated attachments using the app's delete functionality, where available.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage generally removes app-private local data from that device. It does not delete files you previously exported, shared, copied to another location or stored with a third-party provider.

### 9.2 Backups and exported files

You are responsible for deleting backup and export files saved outside the app, including copies in downloads folders, computers, removable storage or cloud-storage accounts.

### 9.3 Advertising and billing records

Google may retain information processed through AdMob or Google Play Billing in accordance with Google's own retention policies and legal obligations. We do not control Google's independent retention practices.

### 9.4 Requests and enquiries

If you contact us by email, we will use the information you provide to respond to your enquiry and retain the correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the request, maintain relevant records or meet legal obligations.

## 10. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the app. The app is designed to use Android's app-private storage for local records and attachments, and Google services use their own security measures for advertising and billing.

However:

- No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.
- Device security, screen locks, operating-system updates and protection of exported backups are also important.
- A person with access to your unlocked device may be able to view information stored in the app.
- Backups saved outside the app may have different security protections.
- We do not claim that every locally stored database or receipt file is encrypted unless the released implementation actually provides that encryption.

If you believe information has been exposed or have a security concern, contact us using the details in the Contact section.
